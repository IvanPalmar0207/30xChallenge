02

The problem

Payables drift when no single rule decides them.

  • 01

    Criteria live in people's heads. The same invoice gets different answers depending on who reviews it and when.

  • 02

    No shared rule, no shared order. Currency limits, caps and thresholds are applied inconsistently.

  • 03

    Decisions leave no trace. You cannot reconstruct which rule version produced an outcome.

06

The engine

A rule that fires first, wins.

One policy version is active per organization. Comparisons are strict and no active policy never means approval. Every decision keeps the exact rules snapshot that produced it.

Blocked ✕

Currency not allowed, or amount exceeds the configured maximum.

currency_not_allowed · amount_exceeds_max
↓
Escalated ▲

Amount above the escalation threshold, or a required field is missing.

amount_exceeds_escalation_threshold · due_date_missing

Approved ✓

Only when every applied rule passes. No active policy? Escalated, never approved.
